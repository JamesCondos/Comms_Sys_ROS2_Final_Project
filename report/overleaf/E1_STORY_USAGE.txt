E1 LaTeX story

Files:
- e1_story.tex: procedure, packet validation, six main plots, interpretations,
  repeat variability, reference/stress findings and summary table.
- e1_story_appendix.tex: five supporting plots with methods and interpretations.
- example_e1_story.tex: a standalone article-class example.
- figures/: the 11 existing single-plot PDFs.

In your existing Overleaf report:
1. Upload the two e1_story .tex files and the figures folder.
2. Ensure the preamble loads graphicx and booktabs (the supplied tau draft does).
3. Replace the old E1 subsection inside Experimental Results with:
       \input{e1_story}
4. After the existing \appendix command, replace the overlapping E1 appendix
   material with:
       \input{e1_story_appendix}
5. Retain your other report sections and appendices.

Both fragments are needed to resolve the main-text appendix references.
Do not add another \appendix if the report already has one.
Figures use normal LaTeX float placement; visual position may move when compiled.
The source places measurement explanations before figures and interpretations
after them. Figure numbers are assigned automatically in insertion order.

For a standalone example, select example_e1_story.tex as the main document.
The article example does not reproduce the tau class's layout.

All measurements come from the existing E1 data; no experiments were rerun.
Packet statements describe scripted analysis of the separate five-minute
validation capture. A Wireshark screenshot is not supplied or claimed.
